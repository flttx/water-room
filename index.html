Goal: find the exit. Four valves open the gate north of the Abyss Baths.

You can hold your breath for about thirty seconds. Creatures in the water track light and sound—crouch, swim slowly, and hide behind pillars and walls.
The lit lifebuoy marks a checkpoint. If caught, you wake there.
